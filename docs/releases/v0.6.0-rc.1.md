- Promotes canonical product, Rust crate, Hermes plugin/payload, browser-gate, current evidence, package, workflow, and current-documentation identities from `0.6.0-beta.1` to `0.6.0-rc.1`.

## Limitations

- Passive detection and redacted local evidence only: no prevention, guard mode, containment, quarantine, approval, or blocking.
- No production support commitment and no universal detection or safety guarantee.
- Autonomous Hermes enrollment remains unproven and blocked until its mandatory disposable clean-host gate passes. The literal operational verdict remains `S3_ADAPTER_BLOCK`.
- The Hermes 0.20.0 browser gate proves read-only plugin activation and browser compatibility only; it does not authorize autonomous enrollment.
- Signing, SBOM, provenance, broader platform validation, retention/purge, outbound delivery, and repeatable runtime upgrade/rollback proof remain outside this release candidate.
